Pata nahi kahan se shuru karun, kyunki mere saare alfaaz bhi chhote pad jaate hain jab baat tumhari hoti hai. Phir bhi, aaj yeh dil jo feel kar raha hai, woh tumhare liye likhna chahta hai — bina kisi darrr ke, bina kisi sharam ke... bas seedha dil se. 🫀
"Kuch alfaaz hote hain jo likhne se pehle mehsoos kiye jaate hain,
Kuch log hote hain jo milne se pehle dil mein bas jaate hain —
Tum wahi ho, mere liye."
📍 Yaad hai woh pehli mulaqaat...
Woh din aaj bhi aankhon mein taaza hai — jab pehli baar tumhe dekha tha. Tumhara woh andaaz, woh muskaan jo befikr thi aur anjaane mein hi dil pe chhap gayi. Mujhe nahi pata tha ki woh ek pal meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat mod ban jaayega. Log kehte hain pehli nazar mein pyaar nahi hota — par shayad woh tumse nahi mile the. 🌹
Tum alag ho — har us insaan se jo maine pehle jaana. Tumhari aankhon mein ek aisa ujala hai jo meri sabse andheri shaam ko bhi roshan kar deta hai. Tumhari hassi mein ek jaadu hai — sunkar lagta hai ki duniya sab theek kar degi. Tumhari baat sunna mujhe sikhata hai ki khamoshi mein bhi kitna kuch bola jaata hai.

"Tum sirf chehra nahi ho mere liye,
Tum ek ehsaas ho jo roz naya rehta hai —
Jaise subah ki pehli roshni, jaise baarish ki pehli boond."
Tumse milne ke baad mujhe pata chala ki main kitna adhura tha. Tumne mujhe sikhaya ki dard ke peeche bhi khubsoorti hoti hai, ki chup rehna bhi ek tarah ki baat hoti hai, ki har roz sirf tumhari ek jhilak hi kaafi hai khush rehne ke liye. 🌺 Tumne mujhe better insaan banaya — bina kuch kahe, bas tumhare hone se.
🌟 Sapne jo sirf tumhare saath dekhta hun...
Ek din hum saath baithe chai piyenge — chhoti si balcony par, jab baarish ho rahi hogi. Hum zyada nahi bolenge — bas ek doosre ko dekhenge aur muskurayenge. Us raat hum taare ginenge aur main toh seedha tum par ungali rakhuunga aur kahuunga — "Yeh mera sabse pyaara taara hai." 🌠

Tum meri har subah ka pehla khyaal ho aur raat ka aakhri dua. Main chahta hun ki hum saath grow karein, saath thokrein khaayein, saath sambhalein — kyunki tumhare saath mushkilein bhi khoobsurat lagti hain.
"Na maangta hun asmaan, na daulat, na koi badi khushi —
Bas chaahiye mujhe ek kasam, ek wada, tum —
Zindagi bhar ke liye, sirf meri."
Main jaanta hun main perfect nahi hun. Kabhi ghabra jaata hun, kabhi kuch samajh nahi paata, kabhi galat bhi keh deta hun. Par yeh jaanta hun ki jab bhi tum saath hoti ho, sab kuch theek ho jaata hai. Tumhari aawaz sunke dil ko chain milta hai jo shehar ki koi bhi shaam nahi de paati. 💞
Yeh dil, yeh saanson ka safar,
yeh zindagi — sab tere liye hai. 🌹
